How it works

A typical week, top to bottom. Tap a section to open it.

1 · Ask who's in Sign-up link

From Home, tap Sign-up link. Type your message, like "Golf Thursday?", pick the date, tee time and course, then Next: send it.

Send to your group chat opens Share. Pick Messages and your golf group text. Everyone gets your message plus a link with the "Are you in?" picture.

Tapping the link shows every player with In / Out buttons. Each person taps their own. They can also mark a buddy (it asks first and shows who did it). New people can add their name.

With alerts on for your phone, you get a notification each time someone answers ("✅ Eddie is in · 7 in, 1 out"). Tap it to open Who's playing. Turn it off under Alerts › Sign-up answers.

Plans change? On Who's playing: Text everyone who's in, Start over (clears answers) or Cancel round (the link then says it's cancelled).

2 · Make the teams Who's playing

Who's playing is your own checklist. Everyone who tapped In is already checked. Check or uncheck anyone else.

Pick players per team and tap Make teams. The teams come out balanced by handicap, with each team's total next to its name.

On the roster: tap a player's number, then another's, to swap them. Try another deals a different even split. Undo takes back the last change. Clear all starts over.

The sign-up link is for the group. Who's playing is for you.

3 · Start the round New round

New round sets the course, holes, bets, skins and Our Match. Next: Rosters shows the teams (or use Who's playing), then Start round.

Tee times start 10 minutes apart. Change one and the rest follow.

On the scoring screen, Text live links to carts sends each cart its own scorecard link. Share spectator leaderboard is a watch-only link for anyone else.

Players who saved JB Golf to their Home Screen keep using that icon. Each new round, it asks "Which cart are you in today?" and they tap theirs. A cart link opened in Safari asks once if they have the app, and shows how to add it if not.

4 · During the round Scoring

Each cart enters its scores on its own phone. You can score any team from your phone too.

Standings shows the leaderboard, skins, money, cart progress with pace of play, and a list of any scores that were changed.

📣 sends a message to every cart (or one cart). Phones with alerts on get birdies, skins, lead changes and more.

5 · Finish up Finalize

Finalize locks the round, saves it to Past rounds, adds everyone's score to their stats, and opens the results to share.

Finalized too early? Open it in Past rounds and tap Reopen (within 24 hours).

In Past rounds, Share this page sends a link to that round's results with the "Final results" picture.

Players names, numbers, stats

Each player has first and last name, a nickname for the scoring screen, usual tees, a mobile number (green dot when saved) and Usually shoots until they have rounds.

Tap a player for their index, average, best score and recent rounds. The index is handicap-style: the best of their last 20 rounds, adjusted for the tees. It isn't an official USGA index.

Add numbers fast: in Contacts make a list of the guys, export it, then Add phone numbers from a contacts file.

Money season totals

Every finalized round adds each player's winnings: team bets and skins less their buy-in, plus Our Match. Tap a name to see each round.

Settle up is for when everyone has paid up. The season totals start back at $0, and All time still has every round. Nothing is deleted.
